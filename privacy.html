Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

TIKFINDER is a website under development. This policy explains how information is intended to be handled as features are introduced.

Current status

At the time of publication, TIKFINDER's live TikTok integration is not yet enabled. The website does not currently require your TikTok password and does not claim to retrieve private account information.

Information and permissions

If TikTok sign-in and Display API features are enabled in a future release, the website will request only the permissions needed for the feature you choose. Information made available through those permissions may include basic profile details and public videos associated with the authorized account, depending on the permissions TikTok grants.

Use of information

Information obtained through an authorized TikTok connection will be used to provide the feature requested by the user, in accordance with TikTok's developer terms and applicable law. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and security

We do not intend to share personal information except as needed to operate the service, comply with law, or protect users and the service. Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards will be applied before account-connection features are launched. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Retention and deletion procedures for any information processed by future connected features will be explained before those features launch. You may revoke an app's access through TikTok's account settings.

Children

TIKFINDER is not intended for children under 13, or any higher minimum age required by applicable law.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the website develops. For privacy questions or requests, contact the TIKFINDER website operator through the contact method published on the website.
